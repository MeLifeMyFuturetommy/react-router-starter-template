# Future Capabilities Lane

Status: opportunity lane
Purpose: hold future-facing ideas that MLMF may not build today but should not lose.

## Belongs here

- future app categories
- future agent roles
- new tool combinations
- capability-first architecture ideas
- media, education, workflow, and operator support possibilities
- old functions that may become useful in a new context

## Does not belong here

- claims of current capability without proof
- production plans without review
- private source dumps
- destructive cleanup ideas

## Evidence rule

Each future capability should record why it may matter, what has changed, what evidence exists, what is missing, and what would trigger revisit.

## Promotion rule

A future capability moves forward only when there is a current need, a safe test, and a proof path.
