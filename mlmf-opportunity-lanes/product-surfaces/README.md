# Product Surfaces Lane

Status: opportunity lane
Purpose: store ideas for what users see and touch: landing pages, dashboards, explainers, forms, training clips, public pages, and operator-facing surfaces.

## Belongs here

- website surface ideas
- dashboard surface ideas
- explainer cards
- training/media concepts
- public product language
- operator-facing workflows

## Does not belong here

- protected source truth
- private user records
- unverified claims presented as final copy
- production assets without proof notes

## Evidence rule

Each product surface should record audience, job to be done, source inputs, claims made, verification state, and test status.

## Promotion rule

A product surface becomes build-ready only after source-link review and C1 direction.
