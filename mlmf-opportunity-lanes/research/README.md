# Research Lane

Status: opportunity lane
Purpose: store external research leads, platform updates, product signals, papers, tool comparisons, and intelligence markers before they become stack decisions.

## Belongs here

- AI and platform history markers
- OpenAI, Google, GitHub, xAI, Manus, Codex, Gemini, Cloud, and connector research
- paper notes
- product-change notes
- external source cross-checks
- Perplexity-style research leads

## Does not belong here

- unlabelled claims
- copied private source material
- unsupported conclusions
- source-truth replacements

## Evidence rule

Every research item should say what happened, why it matters to MLMF, source checked, proof state, and revisit trigger.

## Promotion rule

Research becomes a stack marker only after C3 labels it and links the evidence.
